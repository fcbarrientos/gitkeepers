## For finding the local database:

Browse to `app.db` (it lives in your OS user-data folder)

### For Windows
```aiignore
C:\Users\<user>\AppData\Local\YourAppName\YourAppName\app.db
```
^^ The subfolders are subject to change

If it's not there yet, **run `python run.py` first**.
